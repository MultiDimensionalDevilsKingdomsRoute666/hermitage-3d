# ADR 0007 · Signature feature: Petersburg Time

- Status: accepted · 2026-10-15 · DEC-007

## Context

A recognizable palace is a commodity; the project's identity needs one culturally specific idea that lands in Phase 0 and stays cheap to run. The palace sits at 59.9°N: white nights in June, a six-hour day in December.

## Decision

Petersburg Time is the signature: the palace lives in the real sky at the real latitude, interiors shift baked light states by the same clock. Core is pure, node-tested code (`src/sky/`), contract in `docs/design-sky-time.md`.

## Rejected alternatives

The Cut (dollhouse section) as the primary feature — needs ≥ 2 interiors; adopted instead as a standing constraint (true coordinates) and the first v2 feature. Rastrelli's Line (ink-drawing transition) — parked; risks reading as a shader demo.

## Consequences

Every later room consumes the same clock (lightmap weights, sun) rather than owning its own lighting model; time is round-tripped through the URL.
