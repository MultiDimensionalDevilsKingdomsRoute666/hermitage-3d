# ADR 0008 · Camera: the museum visitor

- Status: accepted · 2026-10-15 · DEC-008

## Context

Free roam demands collision systems, full surface coverage, and produces uncomposed frames; the project's promise is "every frame is composed" with a bounded asset envelope.

## Decision

A spline rail with composed stops; scroll, drag or arrow keys move along it; pointer/touch gives ±35° of free look; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns glides into cuts. Eye height 1.7 m.

## Rejected alternative

Free FPS roam — collision, full surface coverage, uncomposed frames.

## Consequences

Rails are room data (not engine code); adding a room never touches the camera core. The Phase 0 coordinator holds a static stand-in framing until the rail module lands.
