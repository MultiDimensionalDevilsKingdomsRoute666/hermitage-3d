# ADR-0015 · Antialiasing and the resolution law — re-measured on the reference device

Date: 2026-10-19 · Device: the reference MacBook Pro 2017 (Iris Plus 640), headed, idle, det.
Status: accepted (the MSAA part); the dpr-2 part recorded open with numbers.

## Context

The owner reported the palace degrading with distance («the more far the more blurry») — the
far-field aliasing of the dense thin facade geometry (62 window axes, cornice, figures) sampled
without MSAA; the design doc had parked it as «the afar no-MSAA blur by design». The same session
asked whether the T1 resolution law (dprCap 1, buffer 1,048,576 px — §11's frozen anchor) is too
conservative for retina displays, where even ratio 1.0 stretches 2× and larger windows fall to
sub-CSS buffers (measured: ratio 0.84 at 1512 css / 0.75 at 1728 css).

## Decision

**MSAA on at every tier** (`antialias: true` at context creation): measured free on the reference
device — every gate scene holds the vsync floor (16.6–16.7 ms: the square T1/T0/T2, jordan J4 and
gallery G1 at T1), the CI smoke lane passes on SwiftShader with margins, and the T0 floor is
untouched. This amends the phase-0 «§11: no MSAA» context decision; the golden baselines are
re-rendered once (an intentional visual change, the documented path).

**The T1 resolution raise (dprCap 2, buffer 4,194,304) — measured, decision open.** The
interiors at dpr 2 HOLD the floor on the idle reference device: jordan J4 16.8–16.9 ms across two
runs (an earlier 33.3 ms reading was machine load, not the fragment wall — re-measured idle
twice), gallery G1 16.9 ms, the square 16.6–16.7 ms. The raise is viable per §11's «where
achievable», but the margin is 0.1–0.2 ms — sustained load indoors dips it; the §11 anchor
(«16.7 @ 1080p») is a frozen contract an ADR must amend; the CI SwiftShader det-T1 lane would
render 2560×1440 in software and needs a rehearsed pin; the goldens re-baseline once more. The
auto-tier probe already self-corrects for weak devices (it grades by cadence at T2 surfaces).
*The owner disposes: raise (sharp everywhere, edge margin) vs keep T1 at dpr 1 (the `?q=2`
persisted override is the sharp mode; measured free for the square and the gallery).*

## Rejected

- Keep `antialias: false` — the owner's symptom is exactly its cost; measured free to fix.
- FXAA/SMAA post passes — contradicts DEC-015's T2-only post stack; blurs what MSAA sharpens;
  needs the post pipeline T0/T1 deliberately lack.
- Non-integer DPR (1.5) — uneven pixel doubling on 2× panels shimmers thin geometry.
- Per-scene resolution laws — machinery the tier/probe system already covers.
