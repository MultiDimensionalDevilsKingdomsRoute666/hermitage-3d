# ADR 0009 · Sky: hand-written analytic dome over Preetham

- Status: accepted · 2026-10-15, relay round 2 · DEC-009

## Context

three.js `Sky` is the Preetham model — a daylight model. Below the horizon it extrapolates a look that fails the project's defining light state: the white-night band (solar elevation −8.6°…−4.6°, where Petersburg twilights never deepen past nautical). The sky is the feature (DEC-007), so it needs a controlled twilight continuation, not an extrapolated approximation.

## Decision

A hand-written analytic dome built around the frozen blend rules of `docs/design-sky-time.md` §7: palette knots, smootherstep shaping, a warm lobe that follows solar azimuth below the horizon. Consumes 1 of the ≤ 3 custom-material slots; 2 remain.

## Rejected alternative

three.js Sky as-is — costs no material slot, but visually fails the defining light state.

## Implementation

`src/sky/rig.ts` (relay round 4): one fullscreen-triangle ShaderMaterial, exact per-pixel ray reconstruction, three's AgX / colour-space / dithering chunks applied exactly once in the required order; zero textures, one draw call.
