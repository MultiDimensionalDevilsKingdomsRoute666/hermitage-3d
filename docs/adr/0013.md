# ADR 0013 · Interior environment probe

- Status: accepted · 2026-10-17, relay round 14 · design-staircase.md §5 — reopens DEC-010's punt for interiors

## Context

DEC-010 removed PMREM from Phase 0 because one non-preemptible bake can jank a frame, and the diffuse massing needed no environments at any hour. Interiors are different in kind: the Jordan Staircase's two signature materials — gilded stucco and cut crystal — read flat under pure lightmaps, and architecture.md already promised "one cube probe per room" as part of the interior lighting model. The phase-2 planning round inherited DEC-010's punt and had to settle it by ADR ([docs/design-staircase.md](../design-staircase.md) §5).

## Decision

- **One static cube probe per room**, baked in Cycles (256², dusk state, neutral) and committed as part of the room's assets — an asset like any other: generated by a committed script, content-hashed in the manifest.
- **PMREM is generated at most once per room load, inside the airlock hold** — never in the frame loop, never on clock movement, never on scrub. DEC-010's cadence ban is kept: the ban was on non-preemptible GPU work in the loop, not on load-time work hidden behind a beat.
- **`envMapIntensity` is scaled from the frozen §8 weights** (declared scalars: day 1.0, dusk 0.6, night 0.35) — the probe is hour-static, the intensity follows the sky clock like everything else.
- The PMREM render target (≈6 MB per room) **registers in the disposal ledger and is disposed on room unmount**; the two-room overlap during transit is part of the ≤256 MB texture-memory accounting.

## Rejected alternatives

- **No probe at all** — bakes alone leave the gilding and crystal flat. Kept as the documented flip condition: the r17 golden frames at the three §8 plateaux read acceptably with bakes alone (owner's eye), or the measured PMREM generation exceeds the airlock hold on the reference device.
- **Probe per lightmap state** — 3× memory and state swaps instead of an intensity scalar derived from weights that are already the product's clock.
- **PMREM generation in the frame loop / on scrub** — exactly the DEC-010 failure mode, reintroduced.
