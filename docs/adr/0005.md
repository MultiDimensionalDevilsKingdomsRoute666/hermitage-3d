# ADR 0005 · Interiors: theatre sets bounded by the camera envelope

- Status: accepted · 2026-10-15 · DEC-005

## Context

The real palace has ~1500 rooms. Fully modelling interiors is unbounded work that the camera would mostly never see — the museum-visitor camera (DEC-008) constrains what can ever be in frame.

## Decision

Interiors are theatre sets: the union of the room's rail-frusta (plus margin) is the only modelled area; each room gets interior kit plus ≤ 3 hero meshes.

## Rejected alternative

Fully modelled rooms — the 1500-room palace swallows the project.

## Consequences

Room shells are composed against their rail during room design; moving a rail stop later re-opens the modelling scope for that room, not the palace.
