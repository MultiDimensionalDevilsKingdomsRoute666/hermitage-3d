# ADR 0014 · Lightmap technique verdict

- Status: accepted · 2026-10-19, relay round 17 · design-staircase.md §4.4 — the falsifiable measurement the default was riding on

## Context

§4 shipped technique A (the per-state lightmap-blend patch, custom slot 2 of 2) as the presumptive default with a declared flip plan: measure A against B-discrete (the stock-argmax floor) and B-RT (the per-frame pre-blend ceiling) on the reference device, and flip if the numbers say so. Round 17c first had to find why interiors rendered black under every technique (the round's bisect: the day bake's indirect light is nearly absent at the walls, and one night-tuned white point per room quantised what remained to 8-bit codes 0–2 — ETC1S closed the loss; see §4.4's r17c scalar law). The runtime chain was exonerated layer by layer (GPU decode ≡ the PNG reference row-by-row; ETC1S killed 0.7% of surviving samples; uv1 alive and in range; all three techniques consistent) — the measurement ran on healthy content.

## Decision

- **Technique A stays the shipped technique.** Measured headed on the reference device (MBP14,1 / Iris Plus 640, T1, det day plateau, jordan at stop J1, 10-s HUD medians, the round-13 method): A 16.70 ms, B-discrete 16.70, B-RT 16.70 — the flip threshold (A costs >1.5 ms over B-discrete at J1) is not met.
- **The B-RT harness runs the ceiling by design** (its per-room RGBA16F target re-blends every frame), so its plateau number is the mid-seam worst case; the §4.4 plan's plateau/mid-seam split collapses into one number. It holds the vsync floor at +2 draws / +4 tris over the stock path and 40.6 MB ledger (the RT row, honest) against the <128 MB gate — the ceiling fits the frame budget, which is the strong form of the result: any honest conditional implementation (quantised w, re-blend on drift) is strictly cheaper than what was measured.
- The B-RT harness's amendments are recorded in §4.2 (HalfFloat target, stock-colour scaling, the quads' uv1 attribute, the consuming `lightMapIntensity = 1`, the loud renderability check) — opacity/alpha factors cannot carry scale > 1 and the channel-1 maps need the attribute on the plane geometry.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Flip to B-RT** — the measured case for it does not exist (A holds the cadence; the ceiling holds the cadence; the memory gate holds). Revisit only if the r18+ camera rail adds per-frame work that reopens the headroom question.
- **Flip to B-discrete** — owner-decision only by design; with A free at the floor there is nothing to trade the continuous blend for.
- **Re-bake with stronger energies as the black-interiors fix** — the brightness the owner needs is reachable by the declared look gain over the existing EXRs (minutes, deterministic); an energy rebake (~27 min/state jordan) stays an optional quality follow-up that would flow through the same re-encode tool.

## Limitations

- All medians pin at the vsync floor (16.70 = 60 Hz): sub-millisecond differences between the techniques are below the display's resolution at this resolution/tier. The verdict is "nothing measurable separates them at 60 Hz", not "they cost identical GPU time".
- The measurement is single-device (the reference Mac) and single-tier (T1) — the tier table's other rows are unaffected by this ADR.
